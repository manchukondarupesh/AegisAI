AegisAI Drone Safety and Operations Manual

1. PRE-FLIGHT SAFETY

Before starting a drone mission, the operator should verify the drone battery level, GPS availability, communication signal, sensors, and navigation systems.

The mission area should be checked for potential obstacles and unsafe conditions before takeoff.

2. BATTERY SAFETY

Battery level must be monitored throughout the flight.

A drone with critically low battery should not continue normal operations.

When the battery reaches a critical level, the drone should return to a safe location or initiate a safe return procedure.

3. TEMPERATURE SAFETY

Drone temperature should be monitored during operation.

High temperature may indicate overheating or abnormal operating conditions.

If the temperature reaches an unsafe level, the mission should be stopped or aborted to protect the drone.

4. COMMUNICATION SAFETY

Communication signal strength should be continuously monitored.

Weak communication signals may increase the risk of losing control of the drone.

If communication becomes unreliable, the operator should initiate an appropriate safe-return procedure.

5. MISSION SAFETY

Mission parameters should be verified before launch.

Telemetry should be monitored throughout the mission.

Failed missions should be investigated before repeating the mission.

6. EMERGENCY RESPONSE

When a critical safety condition occurs, safe recovery of the drone should be prioritized.

The mission should be stopped when required by safety conditions.

Important alerts, telemetry information, and mission events should be recorded for later analysis.

7. DRONE RISK MONITORING

AegisAI can use battery level, temperature, and communication signal strength as indicators for operational risk prediction.

The ML Agent can classify the predicted operational risk as LOW or HIGH.

The Database Agent provides current drone status, telemetry, mission, and alert information.

The RAG Agent retrieves relevant information from operational documents and safety manuals.
